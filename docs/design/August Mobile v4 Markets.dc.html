HOW THE IDIOM MAPS

Market → a question with a chance (THE CALL, plus August's reads on the week's events). Yes/No → Higher/Lower, scored against the record, never priced. Market page → the call page with chart, read, rules, activity. Positions → the Pit's sim book. Resolution → the owner's inbox, where each idea gets its trigger level. Ideas show "% of the way" between stop and target instead of odds.
